## About me

I’m Alki, a master’s student with a background in **Mathematical and Computer Engineering** from the **University of Tirana**. Through Erasmus+, I’ve also studied at **RheinAhrCampus, Hochschule Koblenz**, where my coursework has included scientific data analysis.

I like problems where I can test an idea directly. In R, that might mean simulating a distribution and comparing the results with what the math predicts. In Linux, it might mean tracing a failure through a containerized service and checking whether the fix holds after a restart.

### What I’m learning now

- **Scientific computing with R.** Comparing sampling methods, using the bootstrap to estimate uncertainty, and checking the assumptions behind regression models.
- **Reliable services on Linux.** Working through container health checks, persistent data, and what happens when a dependency starts late or fails.
- **AI-assisted development.** Exploring where models help with code review and how tests can verify the changes they propose.
